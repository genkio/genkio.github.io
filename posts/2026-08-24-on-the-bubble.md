# On Bubble

Last week, I talked about my experience with the DeepSeek v4 Flash production release. Its capability, speed, and cost got me a bit worried about my US-heavy index fund investment. However, something happened right after that gave me a slight bit of hope, I would call it.

I have this neighbor, an old lady from Australia named Sarah. I know her because pet sitting is one of the things she does, and she used to help out with my dog. Sarah came to me last week, asking if I knew anything about vibe coding. She wants to build her own dog sitting website, and she had already started, with ChatGPT, copying code back and forth, but got stuck. Considering she's been so kind to my dog, I decided to do whatever I could to help her out.

So Sarah came over with her laptop, and we sat down together. The first thing we did was move her away from the ChatGPT web UI and install the Codex desktop app instead. Then I helped her sign up for both GitHub and Cloudflare inside the Codex built-in browser, so that Codex could have access to them. That was all the preparation we needed. After that, we created a new session in voice mode and basically chatted about what Sarah wanted to do, the three of us, ChatGPT included. By the end of the session, we had a website that handles pet sitting arrangements end to end, with mailing list integration and a custom scheduling system that fits Sarah's exact needs. She couldn't be happier when she left my apartment.

It was the first time for Sarah to witness what ChatGPT can do. When the mouse moved by itself in the Codex built-in browser, she gasped multiple times, calling it a miracle. And it was also my first time witnessing someone outside of the tech bubble I'm in seeing the change LLMs bring to the table.

This experience gives me a bit of hope. Maybe in 20 years, when we've recovered from the bubble burst and I'm starting to draw from my investment, it will all be good.
