# On the 3 Stages of Evolution

Allow me start with a quote the reality show, The Apprentice, one of resource I learnt my English from, here’s how it goes “Location, location, location.” End of quote.

That’s what mattered in the physical world.

Imagine two kids. One grows up in a city, right next to a library. The other grows up in the remote village. Obviously, the city kid has much easier access to knowledge.

Then came the internet. It disrupted physical spaces. Now the country kid can access unlimited books in seconds, and it’s basically free, if you know where to look.

Now here come large language models.

When ChatGPT first came out, science fiction author Ted Chiang wrote in New Yorker, “ChatGPT is a blurry JPEG of the web,” it compresses information. But there might be more than that.

It compresses time. Mind blown emoji.

Back to the kids, yes, now they have access to everything, but access is not the same as understanding. They still have to spend the time to read, filter, and absorb all that information. With LLMs, you can interact with knowledge, reshape it in whichever form you wanted, to gain the essence of the knowledge in hours. Projects that took us months can now can be done in days.

I was told in school that, time is the ultimate equalizer, everyone has the same 24 hours.

Not sure that’s true anymore.

Now imagine the city kid who can afford opus 26, which cost 20,000 a month with unlimited tokens, versus the country kid who can only afford a model with below average intelligence, and, capped usage every few hours. Suddenly, the gap is back again, wilder than ever before.

And to that, I just want to say, countrymen back home, ones working on open source models like deepseek, qwen, kimi or dozen others, please keep grinding. Work 996, distill harder.

Country kids are counting on you, developers who checks their coding agent usage status a few times every hour are counting on you.
