# On Creating, Take 2

Last week, on Anthropic's YouTube channel, there was a new video where Boris interviewed Spotify's Chief Architect. I learned that at Spotify, they're shipping around 4,500 production deployments every single day. Mind-blowing number. I think this could surely be considered one of the peaks of agentic engineering.

I used to be a heavy podcast listener, not anymore though. Spotify was one of those apps I downloaded and tried really hard to play along with, because podcasts and music in one app, who wouldn't want that? But I deleted it after a short while every time, because it just couldn't get the basics right.

Allow me to give you a concrete example. Because I'm cheap and don't want to pay for more cellular data, I prefer to download the podcasts first, then listen to them on my commute. Even though I checked the auto download flag, expecting new episodes of my favorite shows to be downloaded automatically, it never worked. Okay, to be fair, iOS can be tough to work with when it comes to features like this, I get it. So I manually downloaded each episode. But often times, those downloaded shows just wouldn't play if I cut off Spotify's cellular network access while outside.

I don't pay for a Spotify subscription, beggars can't be choosers, so I never complained about this anywhere. But after knowing there are close to 5,000 production deployments every single day, I have to wonder, what are all those deployments even for? So I scrolled to the comments section trying to find out. Turns out I'm not the only one thinking that way. The bigger half of the comments were complaining about how bad the Spotify experience is. I'm sure those are the paying customers, and I stand by them, firmly.

Which makes me wondering, is there a direct link between good software and agentic coding? On the other hand, there's few app I tried in the last few months developed by either indie developers or small teams, they're genuinely helping my digital life in a better way, to the point I almost want to write thank you letters to them. I'm 100% sure they're vibe coding at some level at this point in time.

Last week's sharing was a bit of a downer, I have to admit. I have been clouded by the feeling that there's not really a point in building anything anymore. That cloud has been lifted a little, thank you Spotify. Maybe at the end of the day, agentic coding or not, the ones who win over users' hearts are the ones who care, same as always. Hopefully that's the side we're on and heading towards.
